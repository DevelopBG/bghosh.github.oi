GrantIQ360: AI grant compliance platform

GrantIQ360 is an enterprise-grade, AI-powered platform for evaluating grant proposals. It runs on a serverless AWS architecture and uses several large language models (Gemini 2.5 Pro, GPT-4, Claude Sonnet 4) to process complex grant guidelines and proposals, then generates detailed compliance reports with gap analysis and actionable recommendations.   • Payments: Stripe integration with real-time credit management.

research · IEEE SaTML 2025

TMM-Def: Targeted Manifold Manipulation against adversarial attacks

What is an adversarial attack?

Imagine an AI that recognises animals. Show it a cat and it says "cat!". Now add a tiny amount of noise to the image, invisible to your eyes, and the AI suddenly says "dog", or even "banana". That's an adversarial attack: a whisper only the model can hear, crafted to make it fail.

We use Targeted Manifold Manipulation (TMM) to steer gradients toward carefully planted traps. Each trap has its own class label (yTrap), so attacks that fall into one are easy to identify. Because the detector (TMM-Def) doesn't need a separate model, it stays semantically aligned with the original classifier.

Detection combines three filters: a Trapclass filter, an Entropy filter and an OOD filter.

Results

  • Detects ~99% of attacks, outperforming current baselines.
  • Robust to semantic-preserving and adaptive attacks.
  • Validated on CNNs and Vision Transformers across benchmark datasets.

research · arXiv 2025

Explainable AI via robust nearest-neighbour retrieval

AI models are increasingly used in social media analytics, finance and medical diagnostics, so their predictions need to be transparent. One way to explain a prediction is to retrieve the training examples most relevant to it.

We developed a modified backdoor technique that generates query-specific orthogonal triggers. Each trigger locally distorts the manifold around the query. Nearest neighbours are then the exemplar points that activate strongly under this backdoor.

Highlights

  • Explains predictions by retrieving relevant training examples.
  • Identifies decision-critical regions and feature relevance.
  • Works with both CNNs and Transformers.

research · ICPR 2024 · CVPR 2024 FGVC workshop

Composite concept extraction through backdooring

Humans easily combine separate concepts ("red" + "car"); machines struggle unless trained on that exact combination. Text-based concept learning needs large annotated datasets and usually only generalises at foundation-model scale. As far as we know, no purely visual method could learn from a few examples of individual concepts, let alone their combinations.

We define primary concepts (the object class, e.g. car), secondary concepts (a finer attribute, e.g. red) and composite concepts (both together, e.g. red car), and frame extraction as a contrastive learning problem that uses backdoors as the mechanism.

Highlights

  • Needs minimal supervision.
  • Recognises unseen concept combinations.
  • No text annotations or large datasets required.

research · IEEE ASPCON 2020

Local lightning detection & prediction system

Lightning strikes depend on environmental parameters that are volatile and location-specific. We used the dominant ones (electric field gradient, electric field strength, atmospheric pressure and others) to predict local strikes.

A modified AS3935 IC detects strokes within a set range. A fuzzy inference system then makes predictions from the parameter values, without needing a large historical dataset for each location.

Highlights

  • Benchmark results in local lightning detection.
  • Government-funded (RUSA) project at Jadavpur University.
  • Published at an IEEE international conference.
